Something happens to you. You're thousands of miles from home. Will the people trying to help you have the information they need? Or will they be working with incomplete or outdated information?

Living abroad means your life is constantly changing. Your address, phone number, friends, emergency contacts, insurance, and other important details may change without your family or friends knowing.

3PromisesThailand gives you a secure, convenient place to keep that information organized and up to date.

If you pass away in Thailand, our professional stewardship helps ensure that your designated contacts and authorized representatives, including your embassy or consulate when appropriate, receive the information they need, following verification and applicable authorizations.

Because important information is only useful when it reaches the people who need it.

The Three Promises

Our service is built around three promises.

Promise one

Do everything online

Sign up and manage your 3Promises account from anywhere, anytime. No office visits. No appointments required to get started. Just simple, convenient, and secure 24/7 access through our website or mobile app.

Promise two

We notify the right people and follow through.

After your death is verified, your important information and documents are delivered to your designated contacts. We also make relevant information available to your embassy, consulate, and local authorities when appropriate and legally permitted.

Promise three

You stay in control while you're alive. We provide professional stewardship when you're gone.

We notify the service providers you've independently contracted and paid for, request that they begin work, monitor their progress, and document what has been completed—or remains unfinished. We keep your designated contacts informed throughout the process.
