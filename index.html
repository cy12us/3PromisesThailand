The three promises

The service is built around three promises, not a pile of extra features.

Promise one

Plan everything online

Customers securely document personal details, authorized contacts, funeral preferences, burial or cremation wishes, final resting place, transportation or repatriation preferences, and necessary documents. While alive, they may change instructions at any time with secure authentication and audit history.

Promise two

We coordinate everything

Before accepting payment, we verify customer eligibility and put the required service authorizations in place. Following notification and verification of death for a customer whose coordination coverage is active, 3promisesThailand activates the plan, contacts designated representatives, relevant authorities and institutions, and the principal Thai provider. We coordinate the response, monitor progress and keep authorized family members and representatives informed throughout.

Promise three

Authorized professionals carry out the arrangements

Our principal Thai provider engages qualified independent professionals to carry out the customer’s authorized funeral, cremation, burial, transportation or repatriation arrangements. 3promisesThailand follows the case through completion and keeps designated contacts informed. Physical third-party service charges are separate and explained clearly before enrollment.

How it works

A calm sequence. Nothing activates because a rumor reached us.

1

You decide who is involved

Family participation is your choice. The plan does not assume relatives will take part.

2

You record your wishes

Plans, contacts, and documents stay in your account, visible only to the roles you authorize.

3

We verify before we act

A death notification is checked before any coordination starts. An unverified report does not activate the service.

4

A named provider coordinates in Thailand

The company administrator and the principal Thai provider work from the instructions you left.
