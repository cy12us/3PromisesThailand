The three promises

The service is built around three promises, not a pile of extra features.

Promise one

Plan everything online or using the app.

You can return to your account to alter, modify and update your information, contacts, selected providers, personal messages, digital files and wishes whenever you want.

Promise two

We coordinate everything

Following verification of your death, we activate your plan and notify your designated contacts. Our principal Thai representative coordinates with the appropriate local authorities and service providers. We monitor progress and keep your designated contacts informed throughout the arrangements.

Promise three

Authorized professionals carry out the arrangements

Your selected service providers perform the arrangements and remain responsible for their work. Our qualified Thai representatives independently oversee their work, follow up on problems, and verify and document completion of services.

How it works

Enrollment follows three steps. This page is a preview; enrollment and payments are not open.

1

STEP ONE — CREATE YOUR ACCOUNT

Complete your personal information, identification and Thailand contact details; emergency contacts and notification priorities; authorized representatives; recipients of official documents; a personal belongings inventory with up to five photographs per item; digital account information, designated contacts and file recipients; special requests and personal messages. Review and accept the agreement, then pay the $49.99 signup fee.

Result: Account Active — Coverage Pending.

2

STEP TWO — ACTIVATE COVERAGE

Pay $120 and schedule an appointment with the principal Thai representative and two witnesses. Complete the appropriate authorization documents. After the signed documents are verified and securely recorded, your dashboard displays Coverage Active.

3

STEP THREE — RECORD YOUR WISHES

Choose cremation or burial, a funeral ceremony, your preferred funeral company, transportation arrangements and your final resting place. You can update your wishes and personal information whenever you want.

Coverage Activation

Paying the $49.99 signup fee creates an active online account but does not activate full coordination coverage.

Coverage becomes active only after the Thai funeral representative appointment and all required authorizations have been completed and verified.

Once coverage is active, all three promises apply, even while the customer continues paying installments.

If a customer dies with active, uncanceled coverage, the remaining installments are waived and covered coordination services are performed under the service agreement.

These contract terms require legal approval before accepting payments.
