3PromisesThailand

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

1. Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At 3PromisesThailand, protecting your personal information is fundamental to our mission.

We understand that trust is especially important when living far from home. Our business is built around helping expatriates organize, maintain, and communicate important personal information, with professional stewardship when it matters most.

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your information responsibly, securely, and transparently.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information submitted through our website.

2. Information We Collect

Website Visitors

When you visit our website, our hosting and security services may automatically collect limited technical information, such as:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser and device information
  • Pages visited and basic website activity
  • Information needed to maintain website security and functionality

Waitlist and Inquiries

When you join our waitlist or submit a question, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Any message or question you voluntarily provide
  • The date and time of your submission
  • Your acknowledgment of our Privacy Policy and contact notice

We do not require passport details, banking information, payment information, or private personal documents to join our waitlist.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through the waitlist form.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Add you to our enrollment notification list
  • Notify you when 3PromisesThailand opens for enrollment
  • Respond to your questions or inquiries
  • Provide information you specifically request
  • Maintain the security and functionality of our website
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We will not use your waitlist information to send unrelated promotional communications without an appropriate legal basis or any additional consent required by law.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Your Choice and Control

Joining our waitlist is entirely voluntary.

You decide whether to provide your information and whether to include an optional message.

Without your name and email address, we cannot register you for enrollment notifications or respond to your inquiry.

You may contact us at any time to request removal from the waitlist or ask that we stop sending you notifications.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time, subject to applicable legal requirements.

5. How We Protect Your Information

We take the security of personal information seriously.

Our approach includes appropriate technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and loss.

Access to submitted information is intended to be restricted to authorized personnel and service providers who need it to perform their responsibilities.

We review our security practices as our services develop.

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security. However, protecting customer information remains one of our highest priorities.

6. Storage and Technology Providers

3PromisesThailand uses third-party technology services to operate its website and manage information.

These may include services for:

  • Website hosting and infrastructure
  • Secure database storage
  • Email delivery
  • Website security and monitoring
  • Spam and abuse prevention

Our technology providers may process information on our behalf to deliver these services.

We do not authorize them to use waitlist information for unrelated independent marketing purposes.

7. International Data Processing

3PromisesThailand is being developed to serve expatriates in Thailand through an internationally operated technology platform.

Information may be stored or processed outside Thailand, depending on the location of our technology providers and their infrastructure.

Where applicable, international processing and transfers will be subject to appropriate safeguards and relevant data protection requirements.

8. Information Retention

We retain waitlist information for as long as reasonably necessary to manage enrollment notifications and respond to inquiries.

Our proposed retention period is up to 12 months after enrollment opens, unless you become a customer, request earlier deletion, or a longer period is required by law.

Information that is no longer needed will be deleted or appropriately anonymized according to our retention procedures.

You may request removal from our waitlist at any time.

9. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent personal information.

We may share limited information with:

  • Technology providers supporting website operations
  • Email delivery providers sending requested communications
  • Authorized personnel responding to inquiries
  • Government authorities or other parties when legally required

We limit disclosures to information reasonably necessary for their legitimate purposes.

Joining the waitlist does not authorize us to disclose your information publicly.

10. Cookies and Website Technology

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies necessary for security, basic functionality, and maintaining website performance.

If we introduce nonessential advertising or analytics technologies that require additional notice or consent, we will provide the information and choices required by applicable law.

You may also manage certain cookies through your browser settings.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent where applicable
  • Object to or request restrictions on certain processing
  • Request information about how your data is used or disclosed
  • Lodge a complaint with an appropriate data protection authority

We will consider and respond to requests in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

We may take reasonable steps to verify a request before disclosing or changing personal information.

12. Future Customer Accounts and Document Storage

3PromisesThailand is developing a secure online service through which enrolled customers will be able to organize and manage important personal information and documents.

Future enrollment and account services may involve additional categories of information, including identity-verification details, contact information, personal documents, designated recipients, and personal instructions.

Before those services become available, we will publish additional privacy disclosures explaining what information is required, how it will be protected, how it may be shared according to customer instructions, and how long it will be retained.

Joining the waitlist does not create a paid customer account or authorize the collection of such additional information.

13. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults aged 18 and older.

We do not knowingly solicit waitlist registrations from children.

If we discover that a child has submitted personal information through our waitlist without appropriate authorization, we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services, technology, and legal obligations evolve.

Any updated version will be published on our website with a revised effective date.

Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before making material changes to how personal information is processed.
