HOW IT WORKS

Start simply. Add what matters.

Four straightforward steps to organize important information, preserve your wishes, and prepare for professional stewardship. You can complete your account at your own pace.

Hover over a step to learn more · Click or tap to expand

Sign up securely, verify your passport with a live selfie, confirm your phone number and email, and make your initial $49.99 payment.

Start with essentials such as emergency contacts, financial institutions, personal belongings, and other important details. Add, update, or remove information whenever you wish.

Write to loved ones, friends, or anyone you choose—words of love, gratitude, apology, or things left unsaid. Add special instructions, including how designated people should seek access to important accounts or information after your death, subject to applicable authorizations. You can also attach photos, documents, and other files to accompany your messages and instructions. Edit or delete them at any time.

Keep agreements and proof of payment for services you've independently arranged and paid for. These records help us follow up with your providers and document progress during professional stewardship.

AN IMPORTANT COMMITMENT

Your Three Promises begin on Day One.

Once you've successfully completed enrollment and made your initial payment, your Three Promises begin—even if your account is not yet complete. Take your time adding details. If you pass away before finishing your payment plan, we still honor all three promises.
