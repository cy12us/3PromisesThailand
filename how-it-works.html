HOW IT WORKS

Start simply. Add what matters.

Five straightforward steps to organize important information, preserve your wishes, and prepare for professional stewardship. You can complete your account at your own pace.

Click or tap × to expand or collapse a step

Sign up securely, verify your identity, confirm your phone number and email, and accept the terms. US enrollment method and pricing forthcoming — no paid enrollment in this preview.

Enter your information and choose who you want to receive it after your death. Add more accounts, edit, change recipients, or delete them at any time.

Write to loved ones, friends, or anyone you choose — words of love, gratitude, apology, or things left unsaid. Edit or delete them at any time.

Upload important documents such as property deeds, insurance policies, contracts and personal records. Choose who receives each document after your death.

Create a list of personal belongings not already addressed in your will. Record where each item is located and who you would like to receive it.

We protect what matters today and preserve it for tomorrow.
