HOW IT WORKS

Start simply. Add what matters.

Five straightforward steps to organize important information, preserve your wishes, and prepare for professional stewardship. You can complete your account at your own pace.

Click or tap × to expand or collapse a step

Sign up securely, verify your identity, confirm your phone number and email, and accept the terms. US enrollment method and pricing forthcoming — no paid enrollment in this preview.

Enter your information and choose who you want to receive it after your death. Add special instructions, including how designated people should seek access to important accounts or information after your death, subject to applicable authorizations. You can also attach photos, documents, and other files to accompany your messages and instructions. Add more accounts, edit, change recipients, or delete them at any time.

Write to loved ones, friends, or anyone you choose—words of love, gratitude, apology, or things left unsaid. You can also attach photos, documents, and other files to accompany your messages and instructions. Edit or delete them at any time.

Upload important documents such as property deeds, insurance policies, contracts and personal records. Choose who you want to receive each document after your death, and include a personal note or special instructions. Add, replace or delete documents and change your designated recipients at any time.

Create a list of personal belongings not already addressed in your will. Record where each item is located and who you would like to receive it. Choose who should receive the list after your death, and include personal notes, photographs or other attachments. Add, edit or delete belongings and change your designated recipients at any time.

FICTIONAL SAMPLE · PERSONAL MESSAGE

A few things I want you to know

RecipientSarah Williams
RelationshipDaughter
Emailsarah@example.com

Personal message

My dearest Sarah,

If you're reading this, I'm no longer there to tell you these things in person.

Of all the things I've accomplished in my life, nothing has made me prouder than being your father. Watching you grow into the person you are today has been one of my greatest joys.

I know I wasn't always the easiest person to understand, and there were times when I should have listened more and spoken less. I'm sorry for those moments.

I'd like you to have the old photo albums in my study. They contain photographs of your grandparents, our family holidays and your childhood. I hope you'll share them with your children someday.

Please look after your brother. Stay close to each other, even when life takes you in different directions.

Don't spend too much time grieving for me. Travel, laugh, make memories and enjoy the little things. Life goes by much faster than you think.

Thank you for being such a wonderful part of mine.

All my love, always.

Dad

Back in 2008, I purchased a small piece of land outside Mérida, Mexico. I had always dreamed of building a little retirement home there, but life took me in a different direction.
