The Three Promises

Sign up and manage your 3Promises account from anywhere, anytime. No office visits. No appointments required to get started. Just simple, convenient, and secure 24/7 access through our website or mobile app.

After your death is verified, we work to deliver your selected information and documents to your designated contacts, subject to applicable requirements and authorizations. We also help make relevant information available to your embassy, consulate, and local authorities when appropriate and legally permitted.

After your death has been verified, we coordinate the delivery of your personal messages, special requests, and instructions to the people you've designated, according to your wishes. We also contact the service providers you've independently contracted and paid for, request that they begin work, monitor their progress, and document what has been completed—or remains unfinished. Throughout the process, we keep your designated contacts informed and follow through on your instructions, subject to applicable authorizations.
